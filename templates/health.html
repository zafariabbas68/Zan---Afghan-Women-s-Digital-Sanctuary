New Penal Code Impact on Women's Health

Under the new 119-article penal code enacted January 2026, women face severe restrictions accessing healthcare without male guardian permission. Domestic violence protections have been weakened - violence against animals carries longer sentences than violence against women. Know your rights and emergency options.
