URGENT: New Decree No. 12 (January 2026) - Know Your Rights

The newly enacted 119-article penal code has severely weakened legal protections for women:

  • Legal equality before the law has been abolished
  • Husbands granted "authority" over wives - only visible physical injury is punishable (15 days max)
  • Women require male guardian in court - who may be the abuser
  • Leaving home without permission can lead to imprisonment

🌸 Understanding the 2026 Penal Code

Analysis from Princeton University and UN reports on how these laws affect women

Equality Before the Law Abolished

Article 9 divides society into hierarchical categories (religious scholars, tribal leaders, middle class, lower class) with different punishments.

Women are placed in the lowest category with fewest protections.

Violence in the Home Legalized

Article 32: If a husband beats his wife causing fractures or visible injuries, punishment is limited to 15 days imprisonment.

Violence against animals carries longer sentences than violence against women.

Individual Punishment Abolished

Article 34 criminalizes family members if a woman leaves home without permission - up to 3 months imprisonment for relatives who fail to prevent or return her.

Private Punishment Authorized

Article 4(6): Any Muslim witnessing a crime against Allah has authority to punish the offender - removing judicial oversight.

"Afghanistan's women and girls face extreme gender-based discrimination and oppression that amounts to persecution." — UN Human Rights Chief Volker Türk, Feb 2026
