Our Mission: Empower Afghan Women

Zan is a protected digital space by and for Afghan women — inspired by initiatives like Digital Citizen Fund, Awaaz hotline, and UN‑supported programmes.

We provide verified resources, emergency hotlines, and anonymous support, ensuring every woman can access help with dignity and privacy.
