Our Mission

A dignified digital sanctuary for Afghan women: free education, health guidance, legal rights, and completely anonymous support.

NGO verified Anonymous use

🌸 Why Zan Exists

Since August 2021, Afghan women and girls have faced unprecedented restrictions on their basic rights — including access to education, healthcare, legal protection, and freedom of movement. In response, Zan was created as a digital sanctuary where women can access vital information and support, completely anonymously and safely.

The name "Zan" (زن) means "woman" in Dari, one of Afghanistan's official languages. It represents our commitment to centering women's voices, experiences, and needs in everything we do.

Our platform is inspired by and builds upon initiatives like the Digital Citizen Fund, Awaaz hotline (410), and UN‑supported programmes that have been working tirelessly to support Afghan women.

Our Vision

A world where every Afghan woman has access to knowledge, resources, and support to live with dignity, safety, and hope.
